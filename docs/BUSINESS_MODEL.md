# Syncrate Business Model

**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Overview

The Syncrate Business Model defines how the platform creates value for customers, generates recurring revenue, and builds a sustainable business ecosystem.

Syncrate is positioned as an **AI-Powered Connected Business Operating System**, combining SaaS, Artificial Intelligence, and a Connected Business Network into a single platform.

---

# 2. Vision

To become the preferred Business Operating System for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) by digitally connecting businesses, automating operations, and enabling intelligent decision-making.

---

# 3. Mission

Help businesses spend less time managing operations and more time growing their business through automation, AI, and digital collaboration.
