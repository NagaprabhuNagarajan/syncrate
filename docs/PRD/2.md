# Business Opportunity

## 2.1 Market Overview

Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) account for more than 90% of businesses worldwide and contribute significantly to employment and economic growth. Despite this, a large percentage of SMEs continue to rely on fragmented systems such as spreadsheets, paper records, WhatsApp conversations, and standalone invoicing software.

Existing products solve isolated problems:

• Invoice generation
• Inventory management
• Accounting
• GST filing

However, they rarely solve the broader operational challenge of connecting businesses together.

This creates duplicate work, inconsistent records, delayed communication, and unnecessary operational costs.

Syncrate aims to eliminate these inefficiencies by providing a unified Business Operating System that enables businesses to collaborate through structured digital transactions instead of exchanging PDFs and manually entering data.
