# Module 6

# Product Management

## Purpose

The Product Management module acts as the single source of truth for every item that can be bought, sold, stocked, manufactured, or transferred.

Every other module—including Inventory, Sales, Purchases, AI Forecasting, Reports, and the Connected Business Network—must reference products from this centralized catalog.

No module may create product data independently.

--------------------------------------------------------

## Objectives

- Maintain a centralized product catalog.
- Eliminate duplicate product records.
- Support businesses with catalogs ranging from 10 products to over 1 million SKUs.
- Enable accurate inventory valuation.
- Support AI forecasting and purchasing decisions.
- Allow connected suppliers to synchronize product catalogs securely.

SKU must be unique.

Barcode must be unique.

Inactive products cannot be sold.

Archived products cannot be modified.

Soft delete only.
