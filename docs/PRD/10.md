# PART 10 – Enterprise Business Rules, Edge Cases & Acceptance Criteria

---

# 10.1 Overview

This section defines the global business rules, validation standards, workflow rules, edge cases, approval processes, and acceptance criteria that govern every module within Syncrate.

Unlike module-specific rules, these rules apply across the entire platform to ensure consistency, data integrity, security, and predictable system behavior.

---

# 10.2 Objectives

The platform shall:

- Maintain consistent business logic across all modules.
- Prevent invalid business operations.
- Protect financial and inventory data.
- Ensure complete auditability.
- Support configurable business policies.
- Handle exceptional scenarios gracefully.

---

# 10.3 Global Business Rules

## Organization Rules

- Every record belongs to exactly one organization.
- Cross-organization data access is prohibited.
- Organizations cannot be permanently deleted.
- Organization administrators manage organization settings.
- Organization data must remain isolated.

---

## Branch Rules

- Every transaction belongs to one branch.
- Branches inherit organization settings.
- Closed branches retain historical records.
- Branch transfers require approval.

---

## Customer Rules

- Customer codes are unique within an organization.
- Duplicate GST numbers are not allowed.
- Inactive customers cannot create new transactions.
- Customers with outstanding balances can still purchase if allowed by credit policy.

---

## Supplier Rules

- Supplier codes are unique.
- Supplier deletion is prohibited if transactions exist.
- Historical supplier records remain available.
- Connected suppliers follow Connected Business Network rules.

---

## Product Rules

- SKU must be unique.
- Barcode must be unique.
- Archived products cannot be sold.
- Products referenced in transactions cannot be deleted.
- Product prices are versioned for audit purposes.

---

## Inventory Rules

- Inventory changes only through inventory events.
- Manual stock editing is prohibited.
- Every inventory movement creates a ledger entry.
- Inventory history cannot be modified.
- Negative stock follows organization policy.

---

## Invoice Rules

- Invoice numbers are unique.
- Invoice numbers are immutable after posting.
- Posted invoices cannot be deleted.
- Cancelled invoices remain available for auditing.
- Invoice totals cannot be negative.

---

## Payment Rules

- Every payment references at least one invoice or ledger entry.
- Overpayments create customer credit.
- Partial payments are supported.
- Payment reversals create new reversal transactions.
- Payment history is immutable.

---

## Connected Business Rules

- Business connections require mutual approval.
- Shared documents remain synchronized.
- Both organizations maintain independent internal records.
- Shared records cannot expose confidential data.

# 10.9 Edge Cases

## Customer

- Customer becomes inactive during invoice creation.
- Customer credit limit exceeded.
- Duplicate customer creation.
- Customer merged with another customer.

---

## Product

- Product discontinued during billing.
- Price updated while invoice is open.
- Product deleted by another user.
- Duplicate SKU imported.

---

## Inventory

- Concurrent stock updates.
- Negative inventory.
- Damaged stock.
- Expired batch.
- Warehouse transfer failure.

---

## Invoice

- Payment received after cancellation.
- Invoice edited after approval.
- Duplicate invoice submission.
- Network failure during invoice creation.

---

## Payment

- Partial payment.
- Overpayment.
- Failed payment gateway.
- Duplicate payment callback.
- Refund after settlement.

---

## Connected Business

- Supplier edits invoice after customer approval.
- Connection removed during synchronization.
- Supplier account suspended.
- Customer rejects invoice.

---

## AI

- OCR confidence below threshold.
- AI recommendation rejected.
- AI service unavailable.
- Incorrect product identification.

---

# 10.10 Concurrency Rules

The platform must handle concurrent operations safely.

Examples

- Multiple users editing the same invoice.
- Multiple inventory updates.
- Simultaneous payments.
- Concurrent stock transfers.

Optimistic locking should be used where appropriate.

# 10.12 Acceptance Criteria

The platform is considered functionally complete when:

## Functional

- All mandatory workflows operate successfully.
- Validation rules prevent invalid data.
- Business rules are consistently enforced.
- Approval workflows execute correctly.
- Connected Business synchronization functions correctly.
- AI suggestions require appropriate approval.

---

## Performance

- Business transactions complete within defined SLA.
- Searches remain responsive.
- Reports generate within acceptable limits.

---

## Security

- Unauthorized access is prevented.
- Role permissions are enforced.
- Sensitive information remains protected.
- Audit logs are complete.

---

## Reliability

- No duplicate transactions.
- No inventory inconsistencies.
- No financial data loss.
- Automatic recovery from temporary failures.

# 10.14 Data Retention Rules

- Financial records are permanent.
- Audit logs follow organization policy.
- Deleted records use soft delete.
- Archived records remain searchable.
- Historical reports remain unchanged.

---

# 10.15 Business Continuity

The system must support:

- Automatic retries
- Graceful degradation
- Offline recovery
- Backup restoration
- Disaster recovery
- Event replay
- Queue recovery

Business operations should continue wherever possible without data loss.

# Summary

The Enterprise Business Rules, Edge Cases, and Acceptance Criteria establish the operational standards for Syncrate. They ensure every module follows consistent business policies, validation rules, approval workflows, and audit requirements while maintaining data integrity, security, reliability, and predictable system behavior. These rules serve as the primary reference for development, quality assurance, and future product enhancements.
