# Overview

## Purpose

The Connected Business Network (CBN) enables businesses using Syncrate to establish secure digital relationships with suppliers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers, manufacturers, and customers.

Instead of exchanging invoices through email, WhatsApp, or PDF documents, connected businesses exchange structured business transactions directly through Syncrate.

Every approved transaction automatically synchronizes operational data between both organizations while maintaining complete ownership and privacy of each organization's internal data.

# Summary

The Connected Business Network transforms Syncrate from an invoicing platform into a collaborative business ecosystem.

Instead of exchanging PDFs and manually recreating transactions, businesses exchange trusted, structured business events.

This dramatically reduces operational overhead, improves data accuracy, and creates a network effect where the platform becomes more valuable as more businesses join.
