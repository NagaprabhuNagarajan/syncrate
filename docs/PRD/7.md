# PART 7 - Artificial Intelligence (AI) Features

# Overview

Artificial Intelligence is a core capability of Syncrate and is integrated across all business modules to improve productivity, reduce manual work, and provide intelligent business insights. AI is designed to assist users in making better decisions while ensuring that all critical business operations remain under human control.

---

# Objectives

The AI platform aims to:

* Reduce manual data entry.
* Improve operational efficiency.
* Provide business insights.
* Automate repetitive tasks.
* Improve inventory planning.
* Improve sales forecasting.
* Reduce invoicing errors.
* Enhance customer and supplier management.

---

# AI Design Principles

* AI assists users, not replaces them.
* All business-critical actions require user approval.
* Every AI recommendation must be explainable.
* AI must provide confidence scores.
* AI must operate only within the organization's data.
* AI must continuously learn from business activities.

---

# Core AI Modules

## 1. AI Business Assistant

An intelligent assistant that allows users to interact with Syncrate using natural language.

Example:

* Show today's sales.
* Create an invoice for ABC Traders.
* Which customers have overdue payments?
* Show products running low on stock.

Features:

* Business Q&A
* Dashboard insights
* Smart navigation
* Workflow assistance
* Business recommendations

---

## 2. AI Invoice OCR

Users can upload supplier invoices or scanned documents.

AI automatically extracts:

* Supplier Name
* Invoice Number
* Invoice Date
* Products
* Quantity
* Unit Price
* GST
* Total Amount

Users can review and approve the extracted information before creating purchase records.

---

## 3. AI Purchase Bill Processing

Automatically converts uploaded purchase bills into purchase entries.

Features:

* Duplicate detection
* GST validation
* Product matching
* Supplier identification
* Error highlighting

---

## 4. AI Invoice Generator

Users can create invoices using natural language.

Example:

> Create an invoice for Kumar with 20 Cement Bags and 10 Steel Rods.

AI prepares the invoice for user review before saving.

---

## 5. AI Quotation Generator

Generate quotations based on customer requirements.

Features:

* Product suggestions
* Pricing recommendations
* Discount suggestions
* GST calculation

---

## 6. AI Business Insights

AI continuously analyzes business data and provides recommendations.

Examples:

* Sales increased by 15% this month.
* Customer XYZ has not placed an order in 45 days.
* Profit margins are decreasing.
* Outstanding payments are increasing.

---

## 7. AI Sales Forecasting

Predict future sales using historical data.

Provides:

* Weekly forecast
* Monthly forecast
* Seasonal demand
* Product-wise forecast

Benefits:

* Better inventory planning
* Improved purchasing decisions

---

## 8. AI Inventory Forecasting

Predict inventory shortages before they occur.

Features:

* Reorder suggestions
* Dead stock detection
* Fast-moving products
* Seasonal inventory planning

---

## 9. AI Purchase Recommendations

AI recommends products to purchase based on:

* Current inventory
* Sales trends
* Pending orders
* Supplier lead time
* Historical purchases

---

## 10. AI Supplier Recommendations

Suggests the best supplier based on:

* Price
* Delivery performance
* Product availability
* Business ratings
* Previous transactions

---

## 11. AI Customer Intelligence

Analyze customer behavior.

Provides:

* Purchase frequency
* Average order value
* Payment habits
* Customer lifetime value
* Churn prediction

---

## 12. AI Risk Detection

Identify business risks.

Examples:

* High overdue payments
* Slow-moving inventory
* Low cash flow
* Declining sales

---

## 13. AI Fraud Detection

Detect unusual activities.

Examples:

* Duplicate invoices
* Unusual discounts
* Abnormal payment patterns
* Suspicious inventory adjustments

---

## 14. AI Smart Search

Users can search using natural language.

Examples:

* Show invoices created last week.
* Find unpaid customers.
* Products below minimum stock.
* Purchases from ABC Supplier.

---

## 15. AI Report Generator

Generate business reports using simple questions.

Examples:

* Monthly Sales Report
* Profit Analysis
* Inventory Summary
* GST Report
* Outstanding Payments

Reports can be exported as PDF, Excel, or CSV.

# AI Permissions

Role-based access controls determine who can use AI features.

Supported permissions:

* AI View
* AI Generate
* AI Recommend
* AI Configure
* AI Admin

---

# AI Security

The AI platform must:

* Respect role-based permissions.
* Never access another organization's data.
* Never approve financial transactions automatically.
* Log every AI interaction.
* Protect sensitive business information.

---

# AI Audit Logs

Every AI request stores:

* User
* Organization
* Prompt
* Response
* Confidence Score
* Execution Time
* Timestamp
* Approval Status

---
