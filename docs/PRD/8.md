# PART 8 – Reports, Notifications & Employee Management

---

# 8.1 Overview

The Reports, Notifications, and Employee Management modules provide business visibility, communication, and workforce management. These modules enable business owners to monitor operations, receive important alerts, and manage employees efficiently.

This section ensures that Syncrate is not just an invoicing application but a complete Business Operating System.

---

# Module 1 – Reporting & Analytics

## Purpose

The Reporting module provides real-time insights into business performance through dashboards, charts, downloadable reports, and scheduled reports.

Reports should help business owners make informed decisions without manually analyzing spreadsheets.

---

## Objectives

* Provide real-time business insights.
* Support operational and financial reporting.
* Export reports in multiple formats.
* Enable scheduled report generation.
* Support custom report filtering.
